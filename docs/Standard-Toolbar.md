# The toolbar: words or icons

The bar above a chart, and above its day, holds the chart's switches and its actions.
It comes in two ways, and Settings chooses between them for this browser.

## Words, the default

Every switch says what it does, and the actions gather in one menu.

- **The switches** show their words: Chart and Today; Characters, Ten Gods and Hidden
  stems; Natal and With luck; FI and EN.
- **The topics** under the chart's name keep their icons and say their names: Roots,
  Roles and Relationships, each with its count (Roots 3, Relationships 3). The season
  already said its month.
- **Actions** is one button at the bar's end. It opens a menu of the chart's actions:
  Copy link and Copy as text, then Edit, New chart and Compare. The day's menu holds
  Copy link, Copy as text and Print, then Settings. Each item has its icon and its
  words, and needs no tooltip.
- **A comparison** keeps its own actions in its bar, each with an icon and its words:
  Swap sides, Copy link and Close comparison. Its two charts keep their display and
  Copy as text in their own bars, without a menu.
- The day's steps (the day before, today, the day after) and the luck ribbon's keep
  their arrows and their calendar: they move through dates, and say so.

On a phone the chart's bar is three lines: the page and Actions; the display across the
width; the luck pillar and the language. The day's bar is two.

### The menu

Actions opens its menu with its first item focused, by a press, Enter or Space; the Down
arrow opens it at the first item, and the Up arrow at the last. In it, the arrows move
from item to item and round, and Home and End go to its ends. An item chosen does what
its button does, and the menu closes, giving its button the focus, unless the item sent
it elsewhere (New chart, to the form). Escape closes it and gives its button the focus;
Tab, or a press outside it, closes it. While it is open the page's own keys rest. The
day's Copy as text and Print are unavailable until the day is drawn: chosen before, they
do nothing, and the menu stays open.

## Icons

Every switch and action is an icon in the bar, one press away, as the bar was before
words. Rest the pointer on one, or focus it, for its name and what it does; on a touch
screen, hold it. The actions stand in the bar, one button each, and there is no menu.

## The choice

Settings, under **The toolbar**, offers Words (the default) and Icons. The page takes the
choice at once, and so do this browser's other pages; this browser keeps it, and another
keeps its own. Nothing of it is kept with the account.
